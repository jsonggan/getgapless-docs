## Every action gets a response

People judge speed by what they see, not by what the system is doing. What you show depends on how long the wait is.

<ResponseTimeDemo />

AI responses often take several seconds, so most AI products show the answer word by word. See [Streaming](/docs/frontend/streaming).

<StreamRaceDemo />

## Every screen has more than one state

Designs often show only the ideal case. Real screens spend much of their time in other states, and each one needs a design. For the failures specific to AI products, see [Error and empty states](/docs/frontend/error-states).

<StatesDemo />

## Writing error messages

A good error message says what happened, does not blame the user, says what to do next, and keeps the user's work.

## Perceived speed

How fast something feels matters more than how fast it is. See [Performance](/docs/frontend/performance) for how this applies to AI responses.
