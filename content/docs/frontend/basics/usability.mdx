A usable product does not need a manual. Users reach their goal on the first try without thinking about the interface.

## Use patterns people already know

Users spend most of their time in other products and expect yours to work the same way. Save your originality for what makes your product different.

<FamiliarPatternsDemo />

## Make clickable things look clickable

Buttons should look like buttons and links should look like links.

<ClickableDemo />

## Fewer choices, clearer path

Every extra option slows the user down, because they must consider it before acting.

<ChoicesDemo />

## Clear words over clever words

Labels should say what happens, use the user's words, and stay short.

<LabelsDemo />

## Let people undo

People make mistakes. Undo is kinder than "Are you sure?" dialogs.

<UndoDemo />

## Do not make people remember

Keep the current context visible: which conversation they are in, which documents the answer can draw on, and which filters are on.

<MemoryDemo />

## Test with real people

You cannot judge your own product as a new user would, because you already know how it works.

<UserTestDemo />
